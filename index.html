About Me

The person behind
the pixels.

I'm George Wahballah — a junior designer and developer from Cairo who believes that great design is invisible. When a user completes a booking in 3 taps without thinking about it — that's the goal.

I approach every project by first understanding the business problem, then mapping the user journey, and finally crafting the interface and code. I don't design to impress other designers — I design to move the needle for your business.

Beyond the screen, I'm a voice actor, a reader, a home cook, and a digital marketer. This creative mix gives me perspectives that pure-technical designers simply don't have.
